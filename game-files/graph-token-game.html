How to play

  • Pick up takes surplus tokens from a blue vertex, up to your cargo limit.
  • Move highlights legal neighbors; clicking an adjacent vertex moves there directly.
  • Drop delivers cargo to red vertices. The Fill button drops exactly what the vertex needs.
